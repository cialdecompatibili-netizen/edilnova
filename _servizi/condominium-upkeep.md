For a condominium, order and transparency matter. We prepare proposals and reports that managers and owners can read, and work to agreed timings.

## When you need it
- Reports of leaks or detachments
- Common areas to upgrade
- Documents to bring to the meeting
- Works to plan over time

## How we proceed
1. Check and report with photos
2. Written proposal and quote
3. Works followed by a contact
4. Final documentation

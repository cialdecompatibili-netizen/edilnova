A drone gets where climbing is awkward. Close-up images show tiles, joints and cracks and help decide what to do and where.

## When you need it
- Roofs that are hard to reach
- Tall facades
- Check before a quote
- Check after bad weather

## How we proceed
1. Flight planning
2. Shooting the areas of interest
3. Reviewing the images
4. Report of the issues found

The facade is the most exposed part of a building. Recovering it means repairing what is damaged and renewing the finish to protect it for longer.

## When you need it
- Plaster turning to powder
- Visible cracks
- Deteriorated cornices
- Faded or dirty paint

## How we proceed
1. Inspection and damage map
2. Removal of unstable parts
3. Repair and new finish
4. Access by scaffolding or ropes

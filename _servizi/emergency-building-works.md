When part of a building gives way or threatens to, the first step is to remove the danger. Then you can decide calmly how to repair it for good.

## When you need it
- Fallen or loose plaster debris
- Unstable balcony or cornice parts
- Exposed metal reinforcement
- Cracks that appeared suddenly

## How we proceed
1. We assess the risk on site
2. We remove or secure unstable parts
3. We propose the permanent repair
4. We document every step with photos

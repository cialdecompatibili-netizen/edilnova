Before waterproofing you need to know where the water passes. We look for the cause, then choose the system best suited to the surface.

## When you need it
- Dark patches on the ceiling
- Mould in corners
- Plaster that swells
- Standing water on the surface

## How we proceed
1. Locating the leak
2. Cleaning and preparation
3. Installing the waterproofing system
4. Checking edges and joints

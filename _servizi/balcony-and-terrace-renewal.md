A balcony is renewed from the bottom up: first the structure, then waterproofing, finally the finish. Skipping a step means ending up with the same problem.

## When you need it
- Flooring that lifts or cracks
- Dripping onto the balcony below
- Edges with crumbling concrete
- Rusted railings

## How we proceed
1. Strip-out and check of the substrate
2. Repair of slab and edges
3. New waterproofing and flooring
4. Railing and finishes
